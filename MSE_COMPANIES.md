round-robin basis, prioritising banks, IHI, GO, Farsons, AX, RS2, PG, Premier
Capital, Harvest, BMIT, Mapfre, MIDI, Tumas (most likely to carry senior roles),
and cycle through the rest across successive updates. Always also do the recruiter
layers (GRS, Konnekt, etc.) each round.
